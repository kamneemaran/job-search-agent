## Why Low Scores?

### Analysis:
The SAP Jobs Board and Indeed results scored lower because:

1. **Role Mismatch**: Most roles are:
   - **ABAP/Basis engineers** (technical/development) vs. **MM Consultant** (functional/consulting)
   - **AI Architects** (emerging tech) vs. **Materials Management** (core ERP)
   - **Sales/Account Executives** (business dev) vs. **Consultant** (implementation)

2. **Seniority Mismatch**: Many roles are either:
   - **Too senior** (Architect, Principal, Expert with 10+ years required)
   - **Too junior** (Interns, entry-level with no MM experience)
   - **Specialist tech roles** (ABAP, Basis, SAP Fiori) - Pradeep is MM/EWM focused

3. **Location Issues**: SAP Jobs Board shows many non-Europe roles (Tokyo, Buenos Aires, São Paulo)

4. **Visa Sponsorship**: None of the job postings explicitly mention visa sponsorship (normal for European roles, but affects scoring)
